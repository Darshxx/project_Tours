Our Mission & Vision

Heritage360 is dedicated to preserving and promoting India's rich cultural heritage through innovative digital experiences.

Our Mission

To democratize access to India's cultural heritage by creating immersive digital experiences that transcend geographical boundaries, making these treasures accessible to everyone around the world.

We strive to document, preserve, and share India's architectural marvels, historical sites, and cultural landmarks through cutting-edge technology, ensuring these treasures are preserved digitally for future generations.

Our Vision

To become the world's leading platform for digital heritage preservation and education, creating a comprehensive digital archive of India's cultural heritage that serves as both an educational resource and a tool for cultural preservation.

We envision a world where technology bridges the gap between past and present, allowing people to experience and connect with cultural heritage in ways previously unimaginable.

Our Journey

From a small startup to India's leading heritage preservation platform, our journey has been defined by innovation, passion, and a deep respect for cultural heritage.

January 2020

Heritage360 Founded

Started as a small team of heritage enthusiasts and tech innovators with a vision to digitally preserve India's cultural heritage.

June 2021

Partnership with Archaeological Survey of India

Formed a strategic partnership with ASI to gain access to historical sites and technical expertise, significantly expanding our reach and capabilities.

March 2022

Launch of VR Heritage Tours

Released our first set of immersive virtual reality tours featuring the Taj Mahal, Hampi, and Khajuraho temples, reaching over 100,000 users in the first month.

November 2023

UNESCO Recognition

Received UNESCO recognition for our contributions to digital heritage preservation and cultural education, establishing Heritage360 as a global leader in the field.

April 2024

International Expansion

Expanded our platform to include heritage sites from neighboring South Asian countries, furthering our mission to preserve and share cultural heritage globally.

Today

Heritage360 Platform

Now featuring over 200 heritage sites across India with millions of monthly visitors, we continue to innovate and expand our digital preservation efforts.
